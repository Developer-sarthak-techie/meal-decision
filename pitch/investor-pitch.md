# Meal Decision — Investor pitch

## One-liner

We’re building the **default operating system for “what do we cook tonight?”** for Indian households—turning **diet, pantry, budget, and occasion** into **one decision, one grocery flow, and one feedback loop** so the product gets smarter every week.

## Problem

Urban families waste **mental energy and money** on the same loop: open several apps, argue, overshop, throw food away, repeat. Recipe apps rarely respect **pantry, budget, or festivals** and **don’t connect to how India buys food** today—quick commerce.

## Solution

A **meal decision + planning** layer that delivers:

1. A **defensible suggestion** (veg/non-veg rules, time, spice, budget, pantry when on).
2. A **grocery handoff** tuned for Indian quick-commerce partners.
3. Lightweight **outcome tracking** (cooked / skipped / saved / “made it”) to improve the next week.

## Why now

Quick commerce made **15-minute grocery** normal; the missing layer is **what to put in the cart**. **Price sensitivity** and **festival cadence** make **budget + occasion** a native India story.

## Market

Start focused: **digitally active households** in metros who already use Zepto, Blinkit, or similar and cook **4+ times a week**. Expand to tier-2 cities and **regional languages** once weekly retention is proven.

## Product (MVP → near term)

**In the product today (high level):**

- Large scalable dish catalog with strict diet matching.
- Pantry mode, budget bands, occasion tags, detailed recipe + bread pairing.
- Tonight mode (≤15 min), household allergens, kids-friendly scoring.
- Quick presets (PG/hostel, family veg week, budget week).
- Shareable weekly plan (copy to clipboard).
- Smart cart preview with **search-optimized item hints** for partners.
- Hindi / English UI; voice-friendly pantry capture (browser speech where supported).
- Proof of cooking: **“I made this”** with analytics.

**Next milestones:**

- Household profiles (multi-member), stronger **SKU-level** partner mapping where APIs allow.
- Shareable plan links (deep links), corporate / PG **B2B** pilots.

## Business model

- **Subscription** for families (pantry intelligence, advanced planning).
- **Partnership revenue** where structurally allowed (referrals, integrations).
- **B2B**: hostels, coliving, corporate wellness (menus + budget guardrails).

## Moat (honest)

**Short term:** habit and workflow inside the **decision → cart** loop.  
**Medium term:** **pantry + budget + outcome** data per household **plus** integration depth competitors skip if they stay “content only.”
