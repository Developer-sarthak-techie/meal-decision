# GTM Plan - India Urban

## Launch City
Start with Bengaluru or Gurgaon due to high smartphone usage, dual-income households, and high quick-commerce adoption.

## Positioning
"Aaj kya banaye?" solved in 60 seconds with ingredients and recipe in one place.

## Channels
- Creator partnerships: Instagram reels + YouTube shorts.
- Resident community groups: WhatsApp + Telegram clusters.
- Referral loop: invite a family member and unlock premium swaps.

## Launch Steps
1. Closed beta: 100 households, high-feedback loop.
2. Open beta: 500 households in one city.
3. City scale: paid acquisition with lookalike audiences.

## Content Strategy
- Daily "Dinner in 30 mins" reels.
- "No-repeat weekly menu" templates.
- Regional meal packs (North/South/Quick Tiffin).

## Metrics by Phase
- Beta activation > 55%
- Week-4 retention > 25%
- Referral k-factor > 0.25
