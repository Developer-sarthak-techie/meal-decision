# Monetization and Pricing

## Positioning
Free for daily meal decisions. Paid for advanced planning, collaboration, and convenience integrations.

## Plans
### Free
- Daily suggestions (limited swaps)
- Basic weekly planner
- Standard recipe library

### Plus (INR 149/month)
- Unlimited swaps
- Smart anti-repeat recommendations
- Pantry mode + leftovers mode
- Family member voting

### Pro Family (INR 299/month)
- Multi-profile household preferences
- Budget planner and monthly insights
- Priority delivery partner offers
- Festival/regional premium packs

## Revenue Streams
- Subscription (primary in first 12 months)
- Affiliate/commission on grocery cart handoff
- Sponsored recipe placements (post PMF)

## Gating Strategy
- Soft gate: show locked features but allow trial.
- 7-day free trial for Plus.
- Conversion prompts after user reaches value event:
  - 3 weekly plans created, or
  - 5 successful swaps in one week.

## KPI Targets
- Free to paid conversion: 4-7% by month 6.
- Monthly churn below 6% for paid users.
- Contribution margin positive by partner commerce launch.
